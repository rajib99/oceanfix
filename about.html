Who We Are

Marine Engineering Excellence

OceanFix Marine Services LLC is a premier provider of marine engineering solutions serving the maritime, offshore oil & gas and port industries. With over 15 years of experience we are a trusted partner for organisations that need reliable, innovative and cost-effective engineering.

Our engineers, technicians and project managers bring decades of combined experience in marine systems, electrical and mechanical engineering and safety compliance. Based in the UAE, we respond quickly to client needs while drawing on deep knowledge of the regional marine industry.
