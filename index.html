Who We Are

Our Mission, Vision & Objectives

OceanFix Marine provides integrated and comprehensive technology solutions for the marine and offshore industries — from electrical and mechanical works to fabrication, testing and commissioning — so our customers have reliable technical solutions that follow best international practices and standards.

To be recognised as the best one-stop destination for marine and offshore engineering contracting in the UAE, delivering complete engineering, procurement, installation and commissioning while ensuring the safety of people, equipment and the environment.

To achieve these objectives we operate a Quality Management System applied across all of our core activities, supported by ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 standards for quality, environment and occupational health & safety.
